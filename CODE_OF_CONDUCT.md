# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, technically rigorous, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive in reviews and discussions, critique ideas rather than people, provide enough context for technical claims, respect privacy and confidentiality, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

For conduct concerns that should not be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile.

This policy is intentionally concise for the project's current size and may be replaced by a standard community code of conduct as the contributor community grows.
